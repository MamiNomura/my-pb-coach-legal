What we collect

  • Apple ID — used only to sign you in. We receive a unique identifier, not your email (unless you choose to share it).
  • Videos you upload — stored temporarily for analysis, then deleted.
  • Analysis results — coaching scores, notes, and skill trends, kept so you can see your progress.
  • Profile photo and name — optional, shown only to you.

What we do NOT do

  • No ads
  • No tracking or analytics
  • No selling or sharing your data with third parties
  • No access to your camera roll beyond the video you choose to upload

How your video is processed

Your video is sent to our server, which uses AI (Anthropic Claude and Google Gemini) to find your shots and write coaching tips. The video is deleted after analysis. The coaching results are kept in a database (Supabase) so you can see your history.

Your rights

  • See your data — your history and profile are visible in the app.
  • Delete your data — Settings → Delete account removes everything.
  • Export — contact us and we will send you a copy.

Data retention

Analysis results are kept until you delete your account. Uploaded videos are deleted after analysis completes.

Children

My PB Coach is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. If we do, the app will ask you to accept the new version.
