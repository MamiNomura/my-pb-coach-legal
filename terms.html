1. What My PB Coach is

My PB Coach is a personal pickleball coaching app. You upload a video of your game, and the app analyses your shots and gives coaching tips.

2. Your account

You sign in with your Apple ID. Your coaching history, skill level, and stickers are attached to that account. You can delete your account at any time from the Settings screen, which removes all your data.

3. Subscriptions

Paid plans give you a monthly allowance of video analysis minutes. Subscriptions are billed through Apple's App Store and renew automatically unless cancelled. You can cancel at any time in your Apple ID settings.

4. Acceptable use
